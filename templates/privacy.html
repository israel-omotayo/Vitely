Privacy Policy

What We Collect

When you book a service through {{ business.name|default:"Vitely" }}, we collect:

  • Booking info: Your name, email address, and phone number (optional)
  • Appointment details: Service booked, date, time, and any notes you add
  • Usage data: IP address and browser type, used for rate limiting and security
  • Cookies: A session cookie to keep you signed in (staff only)

How We Use It

  • To confirm and manage your bookings
  • To send you booking confirmations and reminders by email
  • To let you view or cancel your appointments
  • To prevent abuse and spam

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Third Parties

Your data is shared only with services that make the platform work:

  • Resend: Sends booking confirmation and reminder emails on our behalf
  • Supabase: Hosts the database where your booking information is stored

Each service has its own privacy policy. We don't control what they do with infrastructure-level data.

Your Rights

You can:

  • Cancel any upcoming booking via the link in your confirmation email
  • View all your bookings using the magic link sent to your email
  • Request deletion of your personal data by contacting the business directly

Security

All connections use HTTPS. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Login attempts are rate-limited. Booking links use unguessable UUID tokens.

Data Retention

Booking records are kept for business and accounting purposes. If you'd like your personal information removed, contact {{ business.name|default:"the business" }} directly.

Contact

Questions about your data? Contact {{ business.name|default:"the business" }} directly. This platform is powered by Vitely.
